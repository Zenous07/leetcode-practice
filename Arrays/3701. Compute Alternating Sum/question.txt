3701. Compute Alternating Sum

Difficulty: Easy

Problem Statement:
You are given an integer array nums.

The alternating sum of nums is the value obtained by adding elements at even indices and subtracting elements at odd indices. That is:

nums[0] - nums[1] + nums[2] - nums[3] + ...

Return an integer denoting the alternating sum of nums.

---

Example 1:
Input: nums = [1,3,5,7]
Output: -4

Explanation:
Elements at even indices are nums[0] = 1 and nums[2] = 5.
Elements at odd indices are nums[1] = 3 and nums[3] = 7.
The alternating sum is:
1 - 3 + 5 - 7 = -4.

---

Example 2:
Input: nums = [100]
Output: 100

Explanation:
The only element is at index 0, which is an even index.
There are no elements at odd indices.
The alternating sum is:
100.

---

Constraints:
1 <= nums.length <= 100
1 <= nums[i] <= 100
